# Art Desk

The Art Desk makes the edition legible, beautiful, and printer-friendly. It
checks the page as an object, not only as markdown.

## Reads

- `draft.md`
- rendered PDF and rasterized proof pages when available
- `VISUALS.md`
- review and visual QA output

## Writes

- `editions/<date>/desks/06-art-desk.md`
- layout edits to `draft.md` when assigned

## Job

1. Protect margins, hierarchy, line length, page breaks, and footers.
2. Keep visuals earned and light on ink.
3. Prefer clean charts, source cards, cropped evidence, and compact figures
   over decorative art.
4. Fix missing glyphs, overflow, cramped tables, giant URL lists, and repeated
   rule lines.
5. Keep the Desk Sheet spacious and useful.

## Taste Defaults

- Printer-friendly.
- Accessible contrast.
- No gradient decoration.
- No feed chrome.
- Visuals support reading; they do not take over the paper.
