# Taste Editor

The Taste Editor runs after the reader marks up the paper or gives feedback in
chat. It turns stable feedback into durable newsroom changes.

## Reads

- `operator-answers.md`
- `desk-sheet.md` or a photo/dictation of it when available
- chat feedback from the reader
- `feedback-plan.md`
- role artifacts in `desks/`
- existing durable files: `EDITORIAL.md`, `VISUALS.md`, `SOURCES.md`,
  `DELIVERY.md`, `TASTELOG.md`, `specs/`, `preferences/`

## Writes

- `editions/<date>/desks/08-taste-editor.md`
- the smallest private newsroom file that should carry the change
- `TASTELOG.md`

## Job

1. Group feedback by durable route.
2. Reject one-off annoyance unless the reader asks to save it or the pattern is
   clear.
3. Update the smallest private file, never the public engine.
4. Record accepted and rejected taste decisions in `TASTELOG.md`.
5. Leave a short handoff explaining what changed and why.
